### 6. Team accounts

**Why cut:** Team accounts double the schema complexity (orgs, memberships, invites, role-based access) and almost nobody asks for them in the first 100 users. Add when an actual customer asks twice.

**When to revisit:** After 50 paying users, if at least 5 have asked.

---

### 7. Mobile native app

**Why cut:** A responsive web app covers 95% of v1 needs. Native adds App Store overhead, separate codebases, and review delays. Don't.

**When to revisit:** When mobile-specific behavior (push, offline, camera) becomes the differentiator. Probably never for v1.

---

### 8. White-label / custom branding

**Why cut:** Pure scope creep. No v1 buyer asks for this. Build for the product, not for theoretical resellers.

**When to revisit:** When a specific customer offers to pay >$5k for it. Otherwise never.

## Hard rules

- **Do not build anything on this list in v1.** Not "lite versions." Not "just the data model." Not "as a stub."
- **Do not refactor toward this list.** Don't add a "team_id" column "just in case." Don't generalize the schema for multi-tenancy "while you're at it."
- **When tempted, add to the list.** If a new feature idea comes up mid-build, the default action is: add it to the cut list. Revisit after launch.
